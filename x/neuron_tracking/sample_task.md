# IMG_6128 target constraints

These constraints apply to this sample, not to the general annotation workflow.

For `IMG_6128.AVI`, annotate the neurons along the **side/belly of the embryo**,
corresponding to the band inside the red outline in
`photo_2026-10-19_14-30-28.jpg`. Follow that anatomical region as the embryo moves
and rotates; the outline is guidance, not a fixed video-coordinate mask.

Dark marks elsewhere on the embryo are outside this task's scope, even when they
look like easier isolated examples. Exclude the large eye, bubbles, and background
texture. Identify neurons in the target band, separating individual identities
and each body's pixels from its branch pixels. Record unresolved ownership or
boundaries explicitly; an unresolved collection of candidates is not one neuron.
