# Premise provenance

Where analysis consumes each author premise, and whether it records the use. `spec lint`
(<../SPEC.md#spec-lint>) reports a premise `inferable` from provenance only when every read
of it goes through a recording site. Then a missing record means that no fact used the
premise. A recorded use is not a need: the fact it supported may have another derivation.
`--probe-analysis` decides such cases by rerunning the program without the premise.

## What the default lint decides

Without `--probe-analysis`, `spec lint` reports `inferable` for:

- a `commuting_statements` entry that inerted no edge;
- an `admission_overrides` entry that suppressed no violation;
- a `reentry_free_operations` entry no host call consumed;
- `trusted_dataflow_summaries` on a chunk where no havoc was discharged;
- a `purity: pure` hint whose name no statement in its chunk reads.

A consumed premise of these kinds, a read purity hint and every other kind is `inconclusive`:
recorded use is not need, and kinds without complete recording cannot tell absence from an
unrecorded read. `--probe-analysis` reruns without the premise to decide those.

## What records reach

Recorded uses reach statement reports and the purity value of graph nodes. They do not
reach dependency-graph edges, atomic groups or the gate. Edges are derived from
`Purity::is_pure` and effect summaries, which carry no dependencies. So "consumed, but by no
fact the gate decides on" cannot be read from the legacy graph. The attribution "this
premise was needed for this inference" exists only in effect translation validation
(`covered_owner_dependencies`, `relocation_dependencies` in
`realizability/effect_translation_validation.rs`). That check is report-only today. It
becomes gate-deciding attribution when the effect verdict becomes authoritative. The legacy graph is scheduled for deletion and gets
no edge-level attribution.

## Recording mechanisms

- Interpreter: `ExecutionState::record_proof_dependencies` and `consume_proof_dependencies`
  fold `ProofDependencies` into observations. `record_inference_use` and per-kind recorders
  write statement report uses.
- Loader closure: `FactContext::assume` (`inference/iterative.rs`) from
  `loader_closure.rs`, reported in `export_reentry.json`.
- Native census: `native_integrity_dependencies` (`execution_index.rs`), carried by
  `Integrity::Intact`.

## `predefined_globals`

Not completed. Three findings:

- The `node_global` alias is enabled whenever `global` is undeclared, not when it is
  referenced, so it is consumed by construction.
- The census reads it feeds take `&self` and have no recording hook.
- Census host keys never reach statement reports, so the lint has no channel for them.

A premise that holds by default whenever a name is undeclared is better linted structurally:
"the chunk has no free reference to `global`, so this premise cannot matter" rather than by
use-recording.
