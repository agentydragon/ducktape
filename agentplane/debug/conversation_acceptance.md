# Agentplane conversation acceptance

This is the current acceptance matrix for the stacked conversation work. Evidence
applies to the recorded revision and invocation. It does not imply a deployment,
live-data reset, or results at a different source revision.

## Assembled regression

Verification branch `codex/agentplane-conversation-verification` combines peer PRs
without merging them into their review bases. At `e06ea7bc4338`, the fresh
[42-target run](https://app.buildbuddy.io/invocation/45320c5d-df45-4561-ad0b-ff9d3a2bde70)
passed 22 of 24 test targets. The pending-command browser target and three shards of
the thread-browser target failed; acceptance remains incomplete. Worker investigations
cover pending-page readiness, terminal command-stream error reporting, and live scroll
anchors. The visual, unit, bounded-window, API/schema, PostgreSQL/Electric, WAL,
projection/storage/ingestion, bridge and runner targets passed.

Earlier focused evidence below establishes individual behaviors and finite resource
measurements; it does not override a failure in the assembled regression.

## Stack review

are merged into devel. The remaining implementation stack is storage #7540 → lazy
debug #7555 → Electric backend #7563 → frontend #7537 → bounded pending-command
membership #7567. Native harness optimization remains outside this work.

Backend #7563 incorporates WAL recovery #7560, rejected-feed lifecycle #7565, and
native-startup diagnostics #7566. Frontend #7537 incorporates recovery/acceptance
#7564, visual fixtures #7561, and the frontend parts of #7565. Those five follow-up
PRs are closed with successor links; their source branches and historical test evidence
are retained. #7567 is directly based on #7537. No PR was merged into devel as part
of this consolidation.

`1f6e388baf`: changes are limited to the reviewed pytest fixture and concrete Electric
service class, the active-view transport recovery candidate and its unit dependency/tests,
and correction of the terminal-command browser test's subscription isolation/assertions.

[d079cd18](https://app.buildbuddy.io/invocation/d079cd18-af62-48d2-95ee-e6dffb1438ca):
