# Mare Island special taxes

Research toward modelling Mare Island parcels, which are mainland Vallejo's ad-valorem tax plus
City of Vallejo Community Facilities District (CFD) special taxes. No district is modelled yet;
the amounts below are press reports, not the rate-and-method documents a district file needs.

Mare Island is a former U.S. Navy base on Vallejo's western edge, transferred to the City in
1996 and redeveloped (initially by Lennar). Three overlapping CFDs appear on residential bills as
flat, non-ad-valorem special taxes:

- CFD 2002-1, Mare Island Facilities: infrastructure (roads, sewer, remediation tied to the Navy
  land transfer).
- CFD 2005-1A, Mare Island Services: police and fire services, charged from population served
  and building square footage.
- CFD 2005-1B: a secondary services district with little public detail.

CBS San Francisco (2024) reports Mare Island homeowners paying roughly $2,300 a year more than
mainland Vallejo, with about 322 single-family parcels contributing over $3M a year and
commercial parcels the larger share. California CFD taxes typically escalate by up to 2% a year
to a per-category maximum and end on a stated term; each is billed as its own line.
